Cinematic psychological-thriller, 35mm film look, dark chiaroscuro lighting, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, anamorphic bokeh, cool blue monitor glow against near-black shadows.

A young programmer with short brown hair, glasses and a light sweater sits at his desk in a dark room. IMPORTANT: he keeps LOOKING STRAIGHT AT HIS COMPUTER SCREEN the entire time. He never turns his head to the camera or to anyone else. His attention stays locked on the monitor.

The shot: extreme close-up of his hand clicking the mouse, then one smooth continuous tilt-up to a tight three-quarter close-up of his face. His face is lit only by the warm-blue light of the large monitor. He smiles with innocent curiosity as he scrolls kitten and cooking videos. The mouse clicks softly.

Then the camera slowly dollies back and pulls wide in one smooth move. He is STILL smiling at his screen, still looking forward. Only now do we see why: dozens of cold, ruthless men and women in dark suits and ties surround him, looming claustrophobically close above and behind his head — holding binoculars, magnifying glasses, cameras. Wall and ceiling security cameras all point at him. The camera keeps pulling back: the young man at his desk, the suited crowd filling the dim room. He is oblivious, still watching his screen, still smiling.

Consistent faces, wardrobe, lighting. No morphing, no cartoon look. No subtitles, no captions, no watermarks, no text overlays.
