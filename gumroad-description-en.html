I TURNED EVERYTHING OFF IN YOUTUBE.

Recommendations. History. Tracking. All of it.

And then I opened YouTube — and it was useless.

The whole value of YouTube is your own preferences. Ten years of them.

Without that, you're staring at a blank page pretending to be a library.

That's why I built PrivacyYouTuber.

YOUTUBE, WITHOUT GOOGLE KNOWING ANYTHING ABOUT YOU.

A Windows app that lets you search all of YouTube — and keep what you find, locally. No account. No cloud. No tracking. No ads.

Just your own library, on your own machine.

BUILD YOUR OWN VERSION OF A CHANNEL

This is the part that matters.

When you open a channel you already know, you get everything — the full catalogue, not just the first page YouTube decides to show you. Then you shape it. You keep what you want. You drop what you don't. You make it yours.

No more hunting through a feed that was built for someone else.

ORGANISE IT LIKE A LIBRARY, NOT A FEED

  • Categories — group videos any way you think. Assign from the folder icon on any card. Tick a category and its videos open instantly.
  • Tags that inherit — tag a whole channel once, and every video in it carries the tag. Exclude a single video from a tag with one click when it doesn't belong.
  • Notes with timestamps — while the video plays, drop a note at the exact second. Come back months later and jump straight to it.
  • Ratings & pins — five stars for the keepers, pin your most-used channels to the top.

YOUR WORK, SAVED — AND PORTABLE

  • Export your database — every channel, category, tag, rating and note, in one file. Back it up, or move it to another machine.
  • Import anywhere — replace or merge. Your library travels with you.
  • Built-in updates — new versions install themselves, quietly.

ENGLISH OR GREEK — YOUR CHOICE

The whole interface switches with one click in Settings, and remembers your choice. Ships in English by default.

WHO IT'S FOR

  • A craftsman looking for one specific technique — build a category, keep every video that matters, find them in seconds.
  • A researcher working on one subject — one category, everything in one place.
  • A teacher, a student, anyone preparing a presentation — collect the videos you need, in the order you need them.
  • Anyone who has to use a computer that isn't theirs — a work machine, a shared one — and doesn't want to leave a trace of what they watched.

Not a feed. A library.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Deep search — hundreds of results, not just the first 20.
  • Your own library — every search saved locally, with categories, tags and notes.
  • Your channels — add a channel, get its full catalogue, organise it your way.
  • Fast — opens in seconds, even with thousands of videos.
  • Clean — one window, nothing in the way.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

  • It doesn't send anything to anyone.
  • It doesn't ask for an account.
  • It doesn't have ads, subscriptions or a feed.
