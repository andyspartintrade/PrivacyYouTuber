## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH — Product description

```
───────────────────────────────────────────

I TURNED EVERYTHING OFF IN YOUTUBE.

Recommendations. History. Tracking. All of it.

And then I opened YouTube — and it was useless.

The whole value of YouTube is your own preferences. Ten years of them.
Without that, you're staring at a blank page pretending to be a library.

That's why I built PrivacyYouTuber.

───────────────────────────────────────────

YOUTUBE, WITHOUT GOOGLE KNOWING ANYTHING ABOUT YOU.

A Windows app that lets you search all of YouTube — and keep what you find,
locally. No account. No cloud. No tracking. No ads.

Just your own library, on your own machine.

───────────────────────────────────────────

BUILD YOUR OWN VERSION OF A CHANNEL

This is the part that matters.

When you open a channel you already know, you get everything — the full
catalogue, not just the first page YouTube decides to show you.

Then you shape it. You keep what you want. You drop what you don't.
You make it yours.

No more hunting through a feed that was built for someone else.

───────────────────────────────────────────

WHO IT'S FOR

• A craftsman looking for one specific technique — build a category, keep
  every video that matters, find them in seconds.
• A researcher working on one subject — one category, everything in one place.
• A teacher, a student, anyone preparing a presentation — collect the videos
  you need, in the order you need them.
• Anyone who has to use a computer that isn't theirs — a work machine, a
  shared one — and doesn't want to leave a trace of what they watched.

Not a feed. A library.

───────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT IT DOES

• Deep search — hundreds of results, not just the first 20.
• Your own library — every search saved locally, with categories and pins.
• Your channels — add a channel, get its full catalogue, organise it your way.
• Quick — opens in a few seconds.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

• Sends nothing to anyone.
• No account required.
• No ads, no subscription.

───────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT YOU GET

• PrivacyYouTuber for Windows (64-bit)
• License for 1 computer (personal use)
• Version 1.x updates
• Email support

Commercial or business use requires a separate license.

REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · 4 GB RAM · internet connection

───────────────────────────────────────────

ONE CLARIFICATION

PrivacyYouTuber is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YouTube
or Google. It is an independent tool.

I built it because I needed it.

If something breaks: andyspartinart@gmail.com
```
