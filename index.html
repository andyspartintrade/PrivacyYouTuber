❓ How to export from Google Takeout

To get your YouTube subscription list, follow these steps:

  1. Open Google Takeout (takeout.google.com) and sign in with your account.
  2. Click "Deselect all" (above the list) to uncheck everything.
  3. Scroll down the list and check only "YouTube and YouTube Music".
  4. Click the "Multiple formats" button below it, and make sure "Subscriptions" is set to CSV or JSON.
  5. Click "Next step" (bottom right) → choose frequency "Export once" → "Create export".
  6. Wait for the "Your export is ready" email — usually a few minutes (sometimes hours).
  7. Download the .zip, extract it and find Takeout/YouTube and YouTube Music/ → file subscriptions.csv or subscriptions.json.
  8. Come back here, click "📥 Import Subscriptions", pick the file — and done!
💡 The file contains only the channels you follow. Nothing else from your data is uploaded anywhere — everything stays locally.
